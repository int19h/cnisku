About the model

Semantics first, expression second

Opposite poles use separately authored poses: expectation is not an inverted surprise face. Compound attitudes remain separate contributions, and the first receives display priority when features compete. Categories such as ro'a and modifiers such as ri'e transform the preceding expression.

Based on the attitudinal system in The Complete Lojban Language, Chapter 13. Facial mappings are interpretive, not part of the language specification.
